Privacy

How this website handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

1. Who we are

UniBridge (“we”, “us”) creates education forums and career fairs. You can reach us at info@unibridge.ae.

2. Information we collect

  • Forms: your name, institution or school, email address, phone number (optional), country (for institutions), the audience you selected and the message you write.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site.
  • Campaign details: if you arrive from one of our adverts or links, the campaign name and source are sent with your enquiry so we know which channels work.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, only if you accept them: Google Analytics, Google Ads and Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity (which shows anonymous heatmaps of where visitors click and scroll), the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram) and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. They tell us how many people visit, which pages they use, and whether our adverts lead to enquiries.

When you first visit, we ask whether you accept analytics and advertising cookies. If you decline, they are not used. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link at the bottom of every page. A small note is also kept in your browser to remember your choice and to avoid showing you the same notice twice.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or send the updates you asked for.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam.
  • With your consent, to count visits, improve the website and measure the results of our advertising.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us run the website and handle enquiries, under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or where required by law. If you accept cookies, Google, Microsoft, Meta and LinkedIn receive information about your visit under their own privacy policies.

5. Retention

We keep your details for as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterwards for our records, then delete them. You can ask us to stop sending updates at any time.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent, by emailing info@unibridge.ae.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls to protect the information you send us. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last revised.
