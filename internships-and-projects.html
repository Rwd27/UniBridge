HomeInternships and projects

Learn by doing,long before university

Real projects and real workplaces help students discover what they enjoy, and give them something meaningful to show when they apply.

Two stages

The right experiencefor every grade

Grades 9 & 10

Projects

Guided, hands-on projects that turn curiosity into something real, so students find out what they enjoy before they choose their subjects.

  • Research and design projects in areas students care about
  • Guidance from people who work in the field
  • A finished project to show, and a first step towards a portfolio
Grades 11 & 12

Internships

Short, supervised placements that give students a real taste of a career and something meaningful to write about in their applications.

  • Placements matched to the course a student is considering
  • Practical experience of a real workplace
  • A stronger story for university applications and interviews
